Terms and conditions — Response Card

These terms cover the Response Card text message program run by SpunkyChicken. By agreeing to receive these messages, you accept these terms.

What the program sends

Response Card text messages are service messages to people who have been issued a Response Card contact card by their organization. They are:

  • A link to install your card, or to install an update to it.
  • A request to confirm that your device is with you, answered by tapping a link.
  • A set-up code, sent as a picture message (MMS), for adding your card to a tablet or other device that has no phone number.

The program does not send marketing or promotional messages.

How you opt in

You receive these messages only after you give your mobile number to SpunkyChicken, or to the organization that issued your card, and agree to receive Response Card text messages at that number. Agreeing is not a condition of any purchase.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. Messages are sent only when a card is issued to you, when your card changes, or when your device is checked, and are typically fewer than five a month.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Any charges are from your mobile carrier, under your plan. SpunkyChicken does not charge for these messages.

How to stop

Reply STOP to any message to cancel. You will receive one message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages after that. CANCEL, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE and STOPALL work the same way.

If you stop, card links and set-up codes can no longer reach you by text. Contact the organization that issued your card to receive them another way.

To start receiving messages again, reply START.

How to get help
