## The program

SpunkyChicken sends service text messages, on behalf of your organization, to people who have been
issued a Secure Contact Card. You may receive:

- a link to add your card, or an update to it;
- a request to confirm that your device is with you, answered by tapping a link;
- a set-up code, sent as a picture message (MMS), for adding your card to a tablet or other device
  that has no phone number.

The program does not send marketing or promotional messages.

## How you opt in

You receive these messages only after you give your mobile number to the organization issuing your
card, or to SpunkyChicken, and agree to receive SpunkyChicken text messages at that number. When
your number is collected, you are shown this statement:

>
> By providing your mobile number, you agree to receive text messages from SpunkyChicken about your

or have the account holder's permission.

## Message frequency

Message frequency varies. Messages are sent only when a card is issued to you, when your card
changes, or when your device is checked, and are typically fewer than five a month.

## Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Any charges are from your mobile carrier, under your plan.

## How to stop

Reply `STOP` to any message to cancel. You will receive one message confirming that you have been
unsubscribed, and no further messages after that. `CANCEL`, `END`, `QUIT`, `UNSUBSCRIBE` and
`STOPALL` work the same way.

If you stop, card links and set-up codes can no longer reach you by text. Contact the organization
that issued your card to receive them another way. To start receiving messages again, reply
`START`.

## How to get help

Reply `HELP` to any message for help, or contact the organization that issued your card.

## iPhone and iPad

On an iPhone, the link in your text adds your card to Apple Wallet as a pass. On an iPad, it
installs your card in the Secure Contact Card app. After that, changes to your card reach your
device through Apple Push Notification service, not by text, and are not text messages under these
terms.

Removing the pass from Wallet, or deleting the app, stops those updates. It does not stop text
messages; reply `STOP` for that. Apple Wallet, iCloud and Apple Push Notification service are
provided by Apple under Apple's own terms. The [privacy policy](privacy.md#apple-wallet-card-iphone)
describes what Wallet and the app send.
